The lowest quotation is rarely the lowest total cost. Suppliers may describe the same product while using different materials, packaging and production assumptions.

## Start with one product brief

Give every supplier the same material, dimensions, variants, target quantity, packaging and destination requirements. Without a shared brief, quotations cannot be compared fairly.

## Compare the complete offer

- Unit price and MOQ
- Sample cost and timing
- Production lead time
- Material and finish
- Packaging included
- Defect and rework terms
- Carton size and shipping impact

## Approve a physical sample

A physical sample provides a stronger reference for appearance, function, dimensions and packaging. Record the approved details before repeat production begins.

## Include fulfillment in the decision

A cheaper product can become more expensive if it needs rework, oversized packaging or special handling. Compare the complete path from factory to customer.
