There is no single best shipping route for every order. The right option depends on destination, product and the customer promise.

## Start with the parcel profile

Record weight, dimensions, product type, declared value and any battery, liquid or restricted-material considerations.

## Match the customer promise

Premium products or urgent replacements may justify a faster service. Lower-value items may need a more economical tracked route.

## Check tracking quality

Define how tracking numbers return to the store or order system and whether status updates are useful for customer support.

## Review performance by market

Delivery results vary by destination. Review recurring delays, address issues and parcel exceptions by country.
