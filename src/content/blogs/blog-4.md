Branded packaging should improve the customer experience without tying up unnecessary cash or warehouse space.

## Begin with high-impact details

A printed insert, product label or branded sticker can create a meaningful change with less complexity than a fully custom box.

## Review four inputs

1. Customer impact
2. Minimum order quantity
3. Production lead time
4. Storage and shipping dimensions

## Approve the physical result

Review a physical sample before committing to repeat quantities. Screen colors, materials and print finishes can look different in production.

## Connect packaging to fulfillment

The warehouse should know which insert, label or mailer applies to each SKU and destination.
