Direct supplier shipping is useful for testing products. As volume and SKU count grow, inventory and order handling often need a separate operating layer.

## Signs you may need a 3PL

- Several suppliers feed the same store
- Brand inserts or custom packaging are used
- Bundles combine multiple SKUs
- Daily order files are difficult to manage
- Supplier production and customer dispatch need different schedules

## What changes with a 3PL

Factories deliver stock in batches. The warehouse receives, identifies and stores it by SKU. Customer orders are picked, packed and dispatched individually using agreed rules.

## Information to prepare

Share SKU count, product dimensions, expected units, daily orders, inbound frequency, bundles, packaging materials and destination markets.
