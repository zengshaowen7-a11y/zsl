## Company media still required

The remaining labeled material slots intentionally use distinct code-native visuals instead of repeated stock photography. Replace them only with company-owned, approved media:

- founder or leadership portrait and factual founding story;
- real warehouse wide shot;
- quality-inspection close-up;
- order journey video from shelf to dispatch;
- daily dispatch photograph;
- team group photograph and named role portraits;
- two client-approved case studies with verifiable metrics;
- independent review, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, YouTube or Vimeo links.

Brand logo files are intentionally reused across navigation and metadata and are excluded from content-media uniqueness checks.
