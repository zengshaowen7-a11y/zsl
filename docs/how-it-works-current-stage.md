# How It Works — current stage selector

## Design

The repeated three-card grid is replaced by an operational stage selector. The heading remains above the module; a vertical stage control sits beside one large shared workbench so users can compare paths without scanning repeated card chrome.

## Garden

Three existing JW operational photographs map to product sourcing, quality control, and dispatch. No decorative imagery or fabricated data was added.

## Taste

- The first stage is selected by default.
- Click, arrow keys, Home, and End switch the active stage and photograph.
- The active stage expands its supporting explanation.
- On entry, the heading and stage choices rise sequentially while the workbench eases in from the right.
- Image changes use a short crossfade and scale transition.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable all animation.
- The existing green section background, container gutters, and Manrope typography remain intact.
- The title uses `clamp(28px, 4.45cqw, 32px)`, `1.12` line height, and black text.
