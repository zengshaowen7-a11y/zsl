# JW Dropshipping Brand Assets

## Identity

- Primary symbol: `/public/images/brand/jw-symbol-clean.png`
- Primary wordmark: `/public/images/brand/jw-wordmark-clean.png`
- Brand source assets: `/public/images/brand/`
- Logo assets must not be redrawn, recolored, or replaced by generated marks.

## Content photography

- Current website media library: `/public/images/jw-content/`
- Source material: frames from the supplied JW promotional film plus six original AI-generated operational scenes.
- Real footage is preferred for warehouse, team, inspection, packing, scanning, and dispatch content.
- AI scenes are reserved for abstract or person-free topics such as inventory planning, supplier comparison, order automation, private-label planning, and global shipping analytics.
- Homepage media uses the `home-*` files. Its 19 image placements intentionally use 19 distinct assets.
- The same asset may be reused across pages only when the sections describe the same operational subject.

## Image treatment

- Content assets are landscape WebP files, normally 1536×1024 or 1920×1080.
- Preserve natural color and the existing green-neutral site palette.
- Use `object-fit: cover` through the existing page components; do not stretch images.
- Keep text, buttons, and important interface controls out of baked-in images whenever possible.
